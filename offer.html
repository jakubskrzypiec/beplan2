Design & Build

How we work

From the first meeting
to the keys.

Six steps, one team. Design and construction run as one process, so nothing gets lost between the drawing board and the site.

  1. 01

    Brief & site visit

    We meet, walk the space and listen: how your teams work, what the place has to do for your business, your budget and your timeline.

    You geta clear project brief

  2. 02

    Space planning

    Layouts and functional zoning turn the brief into an efficient, flexible plan — revised with you until it fits the way you work.

    You getan approved space plan

  3. 03

    Concept design

    The big idea takes shape: 3D visualisations, materials, colours, lighting and furniture, checked against the budget from day one.

    You getvisualisations and a first cost estimate

  4. 04

    Execution design & pricing

    Full technical documentation with MEP, fire safety and IT coordination, approvals and detailed Bills of Quantities for a precise price.

    You geta fixed scope, price and schedule

  5. 05

    Build

    One general contractor on site: all trades, procurement, health and safety, quality control and weekly progress reporting.

    You getone point of contact for everything

  6. 06

    Handover & aftercare

    Testing, commissioning, as-built documentation and the keys — followed by dedicated aftercare and a 24-month workmanship warranty.

    You geta ready space and 24 months of warranty
